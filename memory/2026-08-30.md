# 2026-08-30 — audit of the 0.4.0 candidate

The production-readiness pass closed the gap between observed query pairs and pairs that are safe
to use for inference. Candidate-capped or page-truncated searches remain visible in counters but no
longer enter MRR, top-10, or nDCG effects. The corrected public result is 414 observed / 412
validated pairs across 96 instances; the private one-shot record is 713 / 702 across 12 repositories.
Historical aggregates remain labeled as historical instead of being silently rewritten.

`--hint` remains the chosen task-context mechanism after comparing ten designs: repeatable bounded
literal terms, opt-in, no index or parser, no primary-match expansion, and rejected in unranked
modes. The final public effect is MRR +0.01868 with repository-clustered 95% CI
[+0.00632, +0.03252] and Fisher p=0.00440; nDCG@10 +0.01328 with CI
[+0.00646, +0.02110] and p=0.00030. The private confirmation is non-contradictory, not a second
positive claim.

Review found and fixed four instrument/trust-boundary classes: incomplete pairs entering inference,
unsafe or unreachable answer paths (including symlink escape), prototype-bearing aggregation maps,
and an SWE archive cache key that omitted the extraction-byte budget. Release policy now checks the
exact tag, event commit, ancestry on `origin/main`, and the successful main CI run; formula rendering
binds a one-record checksum manifest to the exact archive bytes.

Final local proof: 67 Rust tests, 33/33 differential cases, fmt, clippy, release build, TypeScript
typecheck, all ten root-tool selftests, ast-grep rules plus positive controls, frozen Bun install,
`bun audit`, the video subproject's frozen install/audit/lint/build, `cargo audit`, actionlint,
shellcheck, Gitleaks, and TruffleHog passed. Browser checks at
1440x1000 and 390x844 exercised search, no-hit behavior, theme, disclosure controls, landmarks, and
horizontal overflow. Two Matt Pocock review lenses and a separate verifier passed after the fixes.
No commit, push, deploy, tag, or release was performed; every new file must be included in the
next explicit commit rather than relying on `git commit -a`.
