# 07 — Which wolf, and what do we call it?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 05

## Question

The public artifact needs a position sharp enough to be disagreed with. Land it.

- **The wolf answer**: the working stance is *refuse the choice* — grep is not bad, it is
  prior-free; the codebase is not bad, it has two different diseases. Pressure-test that. Is
  "both, and here is the mechanism" a real position or a cowardly one? If the honest reading of
  ticket 01 is that grep genuinely is the weak link, say that instead.
- **The name**: "grep hygiene" is the client's phrase and it is good — it is memorable and it
  blames the right party. But hygiene implies cleaning, and half our thesis is *naming*, which is
  construction, not cleaning. Does the term survive, get a second term beside it, or get replaced?
- **The audience split**: the same artifact has to work for a team running it on their monorepo
  Monday *and* for a reader who will never install anything. What is the shape that serves both —
  README with a demo, essay with a tool at the end, or two artifacts?
- **The demo**: what single number or before/after makes the point without argument? modem had
  1,585 → 43. What is ours?

## Done when

The position, the name, and the one demo are fixed, and the concept doc has an outline someone
could write from.
